🎡 Wheel of Finance

Five finance phrases are hidden on the board. How much can you bank?

  1. Spin the wheel, then call a consonant. Each time it appears, you earn the value you landed on.
  2. Buy a vowel for 250 when you need a nudge.
  3. Solve any time to bank your winnings, plus a 500 bonus.
  4. Watch out: AUDIT wipes your bank, and FREEZE or a wrong letter costs a strike. 3 strikes and the puzzle is lost.
